College to Pros Awards Greatness
The Game

Performance, pathways, and recognition

“How do the metrics of player performance, collegiate pipelines, and media-driven awards intersect to define player ‘greatness’ in the WNBA?”

When it comes to defining greatness in professional sports, it goes beyond identifying the player with the highest statistics. In the WNBA, greatness is shaped by combining the athletic performance, development, and the public recognition through league awards and media attention. While points, rebounds, and assists give measurable evidence of a player’s contribution on the court, awards and backgrounds often create an impact on how an athlete is remembered in sports history. Instead, greatness is constructed through multiple factors that include the opportunities players receive, programs that develop them, the recognition that is gained from fans, player statistics, and receiving awards.

The first indicator of greatness is within individual performance. In professional basketball statistics create a huge impact as being the primary method to evaluating athletes across different seasons and eras. In reality, statistical achievement represents only one part of a player's legacy. A player’s path to the WNBA includes their college success and exposure through the NCAA, so this can influence expectations and opportunities before even entering the league. To add on, awards and media recognition shapes which athletes become viewed as icons of the sport. By analyzing the metrics of player performance, collegiate pipelines, and media-driven awards we can explore how different forms of achievement intersect to define what greatness means in women’s basketball.
